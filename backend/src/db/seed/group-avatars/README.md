What the changes mean:

- **scaled:** scaled down to 1600 px width.
- **trimmed:** the picture's own white margins cut off.
- **cropped:** cut to the part that shows the motif — g24 to the staff and wings, g9 to a section of the start page's background.
- **lettering:** the lettering “IT4C” added by the collective.
- **rendered:** rendered from the SVG as a PNG.
- **squared:** placed on a square canvas, white unless noted, so a round avatar shows all of it.
